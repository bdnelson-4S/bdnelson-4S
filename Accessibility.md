      <p class="lede">Ben Nelson is committed to making this website accessible and usable for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.</p>

      <p class="lede"> I strive to design and maintain this website in accordance with generally recognized accessibility practices, including providing meaningful alternative text for images, maintaining readable text and sufficient contrast, supporting keyboard navigation, and using clear and consistent page structures.</p>
      <p class="lede"> Because accessibility is an ongoing process, some portions of the website or third-party content may not yet provide the same level of accessibility.</p>

      <p class="lede">If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have difficulty accessing information on this website, please contact me through the website's Contact page.
